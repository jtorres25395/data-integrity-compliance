## Operational Placement Completion Methodology

Public Arizona DCS reports are accepted as compliant because every child is assigned an allowable reporting category.

This project applies a separate operational test:

**Does the child have an identified placement?**

AFCARS identifies data errors as missing data, invalid data, internally inconsistent data, and tardy transactions. ACF’s broader child-welfare data-quality guidance says incomplete or inaccurate data can cause missed opportunities to identify and place a child with relatives, underpayment to a foster parent, AFCARS reporting errors, inaccurate reports to courts and providers, inappropriate decisions, and confusion.

Runaway/absconded, no identified placement, and missing child are treated as unresolved because they do not identify a current placement provider.

The difference between reported compliance and operational placement completion is the control gap examined in this project.
